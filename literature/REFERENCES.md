# Research Literature References

## Data Center Energy Demand Forecasting

### Relevance to Our Research
These papers provide critical insights into energy modeling, power grid optimization, and cyber-physical resource management, which are directly relevant to our research on data center energy demand forecasting.

### arXiv Papers

1. **Paper 1: 2502.03034v1**
   - Title: Knowledge Distillation from Large Language Models for Household Energy Modeling
   - Authors: Mohannad Takrouri, Nicolas M. Cuadrado, Martin Takáč
   - arXiv ID: 2502.03034v1
   - Link: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.03034)
   - Date: February 2025
   - Abstract: This approach underscores how prompt engineering, combined with knowledge distillation, can advance sustainable energy research and climate mitigation efforts.
   - **Relevance**: Demonstrates advanced machine learning techniques for energy modeling

2. **Paper 2: 2502.05702v1**
   - Title: Graph Neural Networks for Efficient AC Power Flow Prediction in Power Grids
   - Authors: Amir Talebi, Kaixiong Zhou
   - arXiv ID: 2502.05702v1
   - Link: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05702](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05702)
   - Date: February 2025
   - Abstract: Using Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) to solve the AC Power Flow problem in power grids.
   - **Relevance**: Provides insights into advanced neural network approaches for energy systems

3. **Paper 3: 2502.05768v1**
   - Title: Cooperative Optimization of Grid-Edge Cyber and Physical Resources for Resilient Power System Operation
   - Authors: Xiang Huo, Shining Sun, Khandaker Akramul Haque, Leen Al Homoud, Ana E. Goulart, Katherine R. Davis
   - arXiv ID: 2502.05768v1
   - Link: [https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05768](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.05768)
   - Date: February 2025
   - Abstract: Design of cooperative cyber-physical resource optimization solutions to control grid-tied cyber and physical resources.
   - **Relevance**: Explores cyber-physical optimization strategies applicable to data center energy management
